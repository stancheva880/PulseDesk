Getting started

This page applies to every role: how to sign in, how to change your password, and how to switch club, language and theme.

What PulseDesk is

PulseDesk runs a sports club or school. It holds the trainees, the classes, the weekly schedules, the individual sessions, the attendance records and the fees. Each club sees only its own data.

Roles and clubs

Your role belongs to one club, not to the system as a whole. One account can belong to several clubs and hold a different role in each — administrator in one club, customer in another. What you see on screen depends on your role in the club you are currently signed in to.

Role Who it is for
Super Administrator Owner of the system. Access to every club.
Administrator Club manager. Full control of their own club's data.
Employee Trainer. Marks attendance and reads the information they need for it.
Customer Trainee or parent. Sees only their own schedule and fees.

Signing in

As a user I want to sign in to my account

  1. Open the PulseDesk address your club gave you.
  2. Fill in Email and Password.
  3. Press Sign in.
  4. If your account belongs to several clubs, Choose a club appears. Press the club you want to work in. Your role in that club is shown next to its name.
  5. If you belong to one club only, you go straight in. Customers land on My schedule; everyone else lands on Dashboard. A super administrator lands on the new-club form if the system has no club yet — see Super Administrator.

Invalid credentials means the email or the password does not match. Check both and try again.

Forgotten password

As a user I want to choose a new password

  1. On the sign-in screen press Forgot your password?
  2. Enter your email and press Send reset link.
  3. A message says that if an account exists for that email, you will receive instructions shortly. The message is the same when no account exists, so nobody can use it to test other people's addresses.
  4. Open the link in the email.
  5. Enter New password and repeat it in Confirm password. The password must be at least 8 characters.
  6. Press Reset password.
  7. You return to the sign-in screen with Password updated — sign in to continue.
The link expires. If you see This reset link is invalid or has expired, press Request a new link and repeat the steps.

The top bar

The same controls sit in the top right corner of every screen:

Control What it does
Email and role Shows which account you are using and with which role.
Club selector Appears only if you have access to more than one club. A Super Administrator always sees a list of every club.
Theme (icon) One button that cycles light → dark → system. The icon shows the current theme.
BG / EN Switches the language of the screens. Your choice is remembered for next time.
Sign out Leaves the account and returns the sign-in screen.

Switching club

As a user with several clubs I want to switch club

  1. Open the club list in the top bar.
  2. Pick the other club. Your role in it is shown next to the name.
  3. The page reloads and every list is fetched again for the new club. You land wherever your role in the new club takes you.
The list holds only your clubs. You never see clubs you do not belong to. With access to a single club, no list is shown.

Things that work the same everywhere

  • Long lists are paged. The foot of the list reads Page 1 of 4 (87 total), with Previous and Next beside it.
  • Every list has a create button at the top right — for example New trainee — and Edit and Delete on each row. You only see the ones your role allows.
  • Deleting always asks for confirmation and names anything else that will be removed with the record.
  • Save stores your changes, Cancel discards them.

When something is not there

  • A menu is missing. Your role has no access to that section. The role pages list which sections each role gets.
  • An empty screen saying "No club selected". This is for Super Administrators — pick a club in the top bar.
  • A button is not there. Read-only records are still visible; the buttons that change them appear only for roles allowed to change them.
  • A message about missing permission. You opened an address your role does not allow, and the system returned you to a screen you may use.
  • The connection drops for a moment. You stay signed in and the system tries again by itself. If you reload the page while the connection is down, you see the sign-in page: reload once more when the connection is back, and you return to your club.
