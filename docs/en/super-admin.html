Super Administrator

You have access to every club and the rights nobody else has: creating locations and changing roles.

Read Getting started first. Day-to-day work inside one club is described in Administrator — all of it applies to you as well.

Pick a club first

Data in the system belongs to clubs. So your first action after signing in is to pick a club from the list in the top bar. Until you do, screens show No club selected and invite you to select one. The first option in the list reads — select a club —. This applies when at least one club exists. If the system has no club yet, PulseDesk opens the new-club form instead — see story 2 below.

After you pick, every list is fetched again for that club. Switching club works the same way, at any time.

What you see

You see every menu: Dashboard, Locations, Classes, Schedules, Sessions, Trainees, Fees and Users — with full rights in each of them, for the club you selected. Location restrictions do not apply to you.

What you can do that an administrator cannot

Action Why it sits with you
Create a club, with its first location and administrator A new club is a new customer of the system. You are the only role that works outside the boundary of a single club.
Create and edit locations Locations are the base that classes, schedules and location-based access all stand on, so they are managed centrally.
Change an existing user's role Promoting someone to administrator grants full control of a club. That call is yours.
Create a user with the administrator or super administrator role An administrator can only add employees and customers.
Delete an account outright An administrator only removes a person from their own club. Deleting the account itself is yours.
Work in any club An administrator sees only the clubs they belong to.
You create a new club in the application. A + button sits next to the club list in the top bar. It opens the new-club form, which creates the club, its first location and its administrator in one step — see story 2 below.
On a new system the form opens by itself. If no club exists yet, PulseDesk sends you to the new-club form directly after you sign in. You do not press the + button. The menus do not open other screens until the first club exists — they return you to the form. After you save the club, every screen opens as usual.

Step by step

1. As a super administrator I want to work in one specific club

  1. Sign in.
  2. Open the club list in the top bar.
  3. Pick the club. Its short name is shown beside the name.
  4. The page reloads. From then on every create, edit and delete applies to that club.
Check the selected club before you change anything. The same screen looks identical for every club. The list in the top bar always shows which club you are working in.

2. As a super administrator I want to create a new club

  1. Press the + button next to the club list in the top bar. If the system has no club yet, the form is already open. Go to step 2.
  2. Fill in the Club name — this is how people see it in the system.
  3. Fill in the Identifier — a short name of lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, for example sofia-judo. If a club already uses that identifier, the form tells you to pick another.
  4. Fill in the First location and, if you like, its address. Every club needs somewhere to train, and an administrator sees the data of their own locations only — which is why the location is created with the club.
  5. Fill in the Administrator email and optionally a name. You do not choose a password — the person sets their own.
  6. Press Save.
  7. The club is created and you land inside it — it is already selected in the top bar.
If the email already has an account, it gains administrator access to the new club and its password stays unchanged. That is how one person runs several clubs with one account. An email is sent telling them about the new club, with no link — they sign in as usual. A super administrator account cannot become a club administrator.

The new administrator receives an email with a link that lets them choose their own password. The link is valid for 48 hours and works once. Until they open it, the account appears as Pending invite in Users, and they cannot sign in. If the email does not arrive, open Users and press Resend invite on that row.

3. As a super administrator I want to add a location

  1. Select the club the location belongs to.
  2. Open Locations and press New location.
  3. Fill in Name and Address.
  4. Leave Active ticked.
  5. Press Save.

A location with this name already exists means this club already has a location with the same name. Pick a different one.

For a hall that closes: open Edit and clear Active. History is kept, and the location drops out of the choices for new classes and sessions.

4. As a super administrator I want to create an administrator for a club

  1. Select the club.
  2. Open Users and press New user.
  3. Fill in Email. You do not choose a password — the person sets their own.
  4. Fill in First name and Last name, and Phone if you have it.
  5. Set Role to Administrator.
  6. Choose the Locations they are responsible for. Press the field to see the list, or type to make it shorter, then press a location. It becomes a chip above the field; press the chip to remove it. At least one is required: an administrator and an employee see the data of their own locations only, so the form will not save without a choice.
  7. Press Save.
The location picker disappears for the super administrator role. That is expected: this role is not limited by location.
The new administrator receives an invitation by email. A link is sent to that address which lets them choose their own password. The link is valid for 48 hours and works once. Until then the account exists but cannot be signed into.

If the email already exists in the system, that account is added to the selected club and its password is unchanged — no invitation is sent, because the person already has a password. A message says exactly that.

5. As a super administrator I want to change someone's role

  1. Select the club the person works in.
  2. Open Users and press Edit on their row.
  3. Choose the new value in Role.
  4. Check Locations — the new role may need a different scope.
  5. Press Save.
The role applies to this club. The same person can be an administrator in one club and a customer in another. Changing it here does not touch their other clubs.

6. As a super administrator I want to delete an account

  1. Open Users.
  2. Press Delete on their row.
  3. The confirmation asks Delete user ivan@example.com?
  4. Confirm.
Consider deactivating first. Edit with the Active tick cleared stops access and keeps the history. Delete an account only when it was created by mistake or removal was asked for.

Day-to-day work

Trainees, classes, schedules, sessions, attendance, fees and payments work for you exactly as they do for an administrator. The steps are in the administrator guide — including the rule for trainees under 18 and the way a fee status is worked out from its payments.
