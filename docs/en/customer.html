Customer (trainee or parent)

You have two screens: Schedule and Fees. They show the upcoming sessions, let you answer invitations, and show what has been paid.

Read Getting started first — signing in and forgotten passwords.

What you see

Menu What it is for
Schedule My schedule — upcoming sessions for you and your trainees, with answer buttons.
Fees My fees — charges and recorded payments for you and your trainees.

If you are the parent of more than one child, both screens show every child linked to your account, each under their own name.

What you can do

  • See when and where the next session is.
  • Answer each session with Confirmed, Decline or Reschedule.
  • Check what has been charged, what has been paid and what is left.
  • Change language and theme, and sign out, from the top bar.

What you cannot do, and why

  • You see only yourself and your trainees. Other families' data is not available to you. If you expect to see a child and it is missing, ask the club to link that child to your account.
  • You cannot change sessions. Times and places are set by the club. That is what Reschedule is for — it asks the club rather than moving the session yourself.
  • You cannot change fees or payments. Amounts and payments are entered by the club. If something looks wrong, contact them.
  • There is no payment in the system. PulseDesk takes no cards. You pay the way you agreed with the club, and the club records it.
  • Your answer is not attendance. Attendance is marked by the trainer after the session.

Step by step

1. As a customer I want to see the next sessions

  1. Sign in. You land straight on My schedule.
  2. Each card shows the class name, the date and time, and the location below them.
  3. If it reads No upcoming sessions yet, nothing is planned. Contact the club if you expected something.

2. As a parent I want to confirm that my child will come

  1. Open Schedule.
  2. Find the session's card.
  3. Under the child's name press one of the three buttons:
Button What it means
Confirmed We will be there.
Decline We cannot come this time.
Reschedule A request for the club to offer another time.
  1. RSVP saved appears and the button you chose stays highlighted.
  2. To change your answer, press another button. The last choice counts.
Answer early if you can. The trainer sees your answer before the session and plans around it. If you see Could not update RSVP, check your connection and try again.

3. As a customer I want to see what I owe

  1. Open Fees.
  2. Each row is one fee: the class, the period, Amount, Paid and Outstanding.
  3. The status is Unpaid, Partial or Paid. It is worked out from the recorded payments — Paid means the full amount has arrived.
  4. The individual payments are listed under the fee with date and amount. If it reads No payments recorded yet, the club has not entered a payment yet.
Paid, but it still says "Unpaid"? Recording is not automatic — the club enters payments by hand, so there can be a delay. Contact the club if the difference remains.

4. As a customer I want another language or a darker screen

  1. Look at the top right corner.
  2. Press BG or EN for the language.
  3. Press the theme icon to cycle light, dark and system. System follows your device setting.
  4. Your choice is remembered for next time.
