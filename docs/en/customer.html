Customer (trainee or parent)

You have three screens: Schedule, Fees and Cards. They show the upcoming sessions, let you answer invitations, and show what has been paid and how many prepaid visits are left. For classes the club has opened up, you can also book and cancel your own spot.

Read Getting started first — signing in and forgotten passwords.

What you see

Menu What it is for
Schedule My schedule — upcoming sessions for you and your trainees, with answer buttons and, where the club allows it, booking.
Fees My fees — charges and recorded payments for you and your trainees.
Cards My cards — prepaid visit cards for you and your trainees, with the visits remaining.

If you are the parent of more than one child, all three screens show every child linked to your account, each under their own name.

What you can do

  • See when and where the next session is.
  • Answer each session with Confirmed, Decline or Reschedule.
  • Check what has been charged, what has been paid and what is left.
  • See how many visits are left on a prepaid card, and when the card expires.
  • Book a spot for yourself or your child — on the classes where the club allows it, and up to the deadline it set.
  • Cancel a booking the same way, whoever made it.
  • Join the waiting list of a full session yourself, and leave it again.
  • Take a freed spot on a full session by opening the link from a waitlist email.
  • Change language and theme, and sign out, from the top bar.

What you cannot do, and why

  • You see only yourself and your trainees. Other families' data is not available to you. If you expect to see a child and it is missing, ask the club to link that child to your account.
  • You cannot change sessions. Times and places are set by the club. That is what Reschedule is for — it asks the club rather than moving the session yourself. Booking is a different thing: it puts you on a session that already exists.
  • You cannot book everywhere. The Book button appears only on classes the club opened for self-booking, only for trainees already enrolled in that class, and only before the deadline. Where it is missing, the club books for you — ask them.
  • You cannot change fees or payments. Amounts and payments are entered by the club. If something looks wrong, contact them.
  • There is no payment in the system. PulseDesk takes no bank card. You pay the way you agreed with the club, and the club records it — visit cards included: buying one is arranged with the club, not in the portal.
  • Your answer is not attendance. Attendance is marked by the trainer after the session.

Step by step

1. As a customer I want to see the next sessions

  1. Sign in. You land straight on My schedule.
  2. Each card shows the class name, the date and time, and the location below them.
  3. If it reads No upcoming sessions yet, nothing is planned. Contact the club if you expected something.

2. As a parent I want to confirm that my child will come

  1. Open Schedule.
  2. Find the session's card.
  3. Under the child's name press one of the three buttons:
Button What it means
Confirmed We will be there.
Decline We cannot come this time.
Reschedule A request for the club to offer another time.
  1. RSVP saved appears and the button you chose stays highlighted.
  2. To change your answer, press another button. The last choice counts.
Answer early if you can. The trainer sees your answer before the session and plans around it. If you see Could not update RSVP, check your connection and try again.

3. As a parent I want to put my child on a session — or take them off

  1. Open Schedule and find the session's card.
  2. Under the child's name press Book. Booked appears and the card now shows the normal answer buttons — the booking is made and counts as Confirmed.
  3. To undo it, press Cancel booking next to those buttons. It works on any booking of your trainee, including one the club made.
No button? Then this class is not open for it. Book shows up only when the club allowed self-booking for that class, your trainee is enrolled in it, there is a free place, and the deadline has not passed. Once it has, the card reads The self-service window has closed — contact the club to change this instead.
A card visit is used, as always. Booking spends one visit from a prepaid card, and cancelling gives it back — exactly as when the club books for you. If nothing usable is left you may still book; the card shows No card visits left — arrange a new card or a fee with the club. Booking is still allowed.

4. As a customer I want to see what I owe

  1. Open Fees.
  2. Each row is one fee: the class, the period, Amount, Paid and Outstanding.
  3. The status is Unpaid, Partial or Paid. It is worked out from the recorded payments — Paid means the full amount has arrived.
  4. The individual payments are listed under the fee with date and amount. If it reads No payments recorded yet, the club has not entered a payment yet.
Paid, but it still says "Unpaid"? Recording is not automatic — the club enters payments by hand, so there can be a delay. Contact the club if the difference remains.

5. As a customer I want to know how many prepaid visits are left

  1. Open Cards.
  2. Each card shows the class it is for (or Whole club), the Visits remaining out of the total, and Valid until — or Never expires.
  3. A card that is finished carries a mark: Used up, Expired or Cancelled. That is the signal to arrange the next one with the club — buying is done at the club, not in the portal.
Visits are spent by bookings. When the trainee is put on a session — by the club or by you — one visit leaves the card by itself; if the booking is removed, the visit comes back. There is nothing to press for it.

6. As a customer I want a place on a full session

A full session shows No spots left. If the class keeps a waiting list — and allows self-booking — you get Join waitlist instead. Press it and the row reads On the waitlist with a Leave waitlist button next to it. Leaving is always allowed, even after the booking deadline; joining is not. The club can also put you on the list itself. Once the session has started the row goes: no place can free up for you any more.

Being on the list costs nothing and uses no card visit — that happens only if you actually get the place. What happens when a spot frees up depends on how the class is set up — you will get one of two emails:

  1. "Your trainee was booked." The first in the queue got the spot automatically. There is nothing to do — the session appears in My schedule.
  2. An offer with a link. Everyone in the queue gets the same link, and the first to open it takes the spot. The page answers at once: The spot is yours! with the class and time, or The spot has already been taken — you stay in the queue and get a new link at the next opening. This link is invalid or has expired means a newer offer replaced it, or the booking deadline of the class has passed.
Very late openings are not passed on. If the place frees up after the class's booking deadline — or after the session has started — nobody is booked from the list and no email goes out. The club fills the place by hand at that point.

7. As a customer I want another language or a darker screen

  1. Look at the top right corner.
  2. Press BG or EN for the language.
  3. Press the theme icon to cycle light, dark and system. System follows your device setting.
  4. Your choice is remembered for next time.
