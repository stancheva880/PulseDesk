Employee (trainer)

Your job in PulseDesk is one main task: record who attended a session. The other screens are there for reference.

Read Getting started first — signing in, passwords and switching club.

What you see

Menu What it is for Rights
Dashboard The screen you land on after signing in. read
Locations The places where classes happen. read only
Classes What each class is, its price, its trainers, and whether customers may book themselves into it. read only
Sessions The individual occurrences, each with a Status of Scheduled, Completed or Cancelled. Attendance is reached from here. Above the list, From, Before, Class, Trainer and Location narrow it — useful for finding one session in a busy week. A Calendar button switches the list to a month, week or day calendar, and Table switches back. read + attendance
Trainees The list, plus a record per trainee with the guardian contacts. read only
Fees Reference for who owes what. read only
Cards Prepaid visit cards — reference for how many visits a trainee has left. read only

What you can do

  • Mark attendance for a session and save every row at once.
  • Add a trainee to a session when they turn up without being on the list.
  • Remove a trainee from a session, and manage the waitlist when the session is full.
  • Open a trainee's record and read their phone, email and guardian contacts.
  • Check the status of a fee when a parent asks you.

What you cannot do, and why

  • There are no Schedules and Users menus. Weekly schedules and people's access are managed by the club administrator. Opening such an address directly returns you to Dashboard.
  • You do not create or change classes, sessions, trainees, locations or fees. You see them to do your work, but the buttons that change them are not for you.
  • You do not record payments. The administrator takes the money and enters it.
  • You see your own locations only. The trainee, fee and location lists cover the locations you are assigned to. A trainee from another hall does not appear — ask the administrator for them. The class and session lists are narrower: they show only the classes and the sessions where you are a trainer. A session in your own hall that another trainer takes does not appear. If you see nothing at all, you probably have no location assigned; the club administrator fixes that.

Step by step

1. As a trainer I want to mark attendance for today's session

  1. Open Sessions.
  2. Find the session by date, time and class.
  3. Press Mark attendance on its row.
  4. For each trainee choose a Status: Present for those who came, Absent for those who did not, Excused when they told you in advance. Anyone you do not mark stays Pending.
  5. Press Save all. A message shows how many rows were saved, for example Saved 12 row(s).
Record it during the session or straight after. Attendance feeds the fees of classes that are billed per session.

2. As a trainer I want to add a trainee who is not on the list

  1. Open the attendance screen for the session.
  2. Pick the person in the Add a trainee… list.
  3. Press Add. A new row appears.
  4. Choose a status and press Save all.

If the list is empty and reads Every active trainee is already on this session, there is nobody left to add. The list holds the trainees of your own locations, so somebody from another hall does not appear — nor does somebody who has not been created yet. Ask the administrator either way.

Cards and capacity. A trainee whose visit card has no visits left is marked no visits left; adding them still works — a warning suggests a new card or a fee, and the money side is the administrator's. Adding a trainee spends one visit from their card by itself; Remove from session gives it back. When the session is full you see No spots left — if the class has a waitlist, put the trainee on it with Add to waitlist, and the system books or emails the queue by itself when a spot frees up — unless the class's booking deadline has passed or the session has started, in which case the freed place waits for you to fill it by hand.

3. As a trainer I want to know what the customer answered

  1. Open the attendance screen for the session.
  2. Look at the Customer RSVP column: Confirmed, Declined or Wants to reschedule. Empty means no answer.
The customer's answer is not attendance. The customer states an intention; the real attendance is what you mark. Pass reschedule requests to the administrator — only they move sessions.
Rows can appear and disappear without you. On a class the administrator opened for customer self-booking, families put their own trainees on the session and take them off again, up to the class's deadline. Such a row arrives already Confirmed. Re-open the attendance screen before you start marking, so you work from the current list.

4. As a trainer I want to check whether a fee is paid

  1. Open Fees.
  2. Type the trainee's name or email address in Search by trainee name or email… — the class name is not searched here.
  3. Optionally narrow the list: Class, Month and Status — Unpaid, Partial, Paid or Outstanding.
  4. Outstanding shows the unpaid and the partly paid fees together. That answers "who still owes for this class this month".
  5. Read the row: Amount is what is charged for the period, Outstanding is what is left after the payments recorded.

The list is reference only. Payments are recorded by the administrator, so send money questions to them.

5. As a trainer I want a parent's contact details

  1. Open Trainees.
  2. The list shows Last name, First name, Age and Status. Use Next if the person is on a later page.
  3. Press View on their row.
  4. A read-only record opens: Phone, Email, and the trainee's classes and locations.
  5. Below it sits Guardian contacts — name, Relationship to the child, phone and email. The one marked Primary contact is the person to call first.
Personal data is for club work only. Do not pass phone numbers or email addresses on to anyone else.
